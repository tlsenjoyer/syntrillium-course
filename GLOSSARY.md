# Syntrillium course

Vocabulary for the Russian adaptation of Syntrillium's introductory audio course.

## Language

**Original course**:
The Syntrillium introductory audio course contained in `course.exe`, used as the reference for the adaptation's curriculum, explanations, examples, and visual material.

**Russian adaptation**:
The Russian-language edition of the original course, with Russian interface text and narration.

**Visualization**:
A noninteractive visual demonstration or animation accompanying the course's explanations.
_Avoid_: Interactive simulation

**Narration**:
Spoken audio that presents a course explanation to the learner.

**Source segment**:
A division of the original course's narration with its associated visual material. A source segment may contain only part of a sentence.

**Narration utterance**:
A complete sentence or short coherent passage spoken as one continuous delivery in the Russian adaptation. A narration utterance may span multiple source segments.

**Candidate narration**:
Generated narration that is available for listening and review but is not yet approved for learner playback.

**Approved narration**:
Narration accepted for learner playback in the Russian adaptation.
